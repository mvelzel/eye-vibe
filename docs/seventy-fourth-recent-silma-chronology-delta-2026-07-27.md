## Cryptography delta

orbit partition, output index, key, or held-out prediction. Non-transitive
examples such as `C5 x C3` demonstrate capacity, not an Eye model.

when the hidden state is fixed. For an 83-symbol GAK its visible normal factor

state-of-the-art contribution.
