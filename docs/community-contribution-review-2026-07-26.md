# Community-facing contribution review

I have not solved or materially decoded the Eye Messages.

The defensible contributions are narrow:

- I matched the 2025 executable's Eye initializer to all 150 packed corpus
  words. Its sole caller passes only `(x,y,panel index)`, ruling out a runtime
  decryptor or caller-supplied key at that interface.
- I located primary developer evidence attributing the Eye-symbol secret to
  Petri Purho.
- A direct visible-state permutation model needs exactly 19 arbitrary actions,
  but every non-pivot class is independently recolorable. Its coloring cannot
  recover unary plaintext.
- The Gate dossier's `12+43+9+8` Veska partition requires an unstated mask;
  straightforward spatial separation gives `11+44+9+8`.

I would not present the `FI/+358`, final-row anchor/phase, quotient-table, or
Veska/Gate numerical patterns as established advances: they are retrospective,
dependent, or lack an independent prediction. The factoradic header was
community work that I reproduced. Most failed model searches are local
exclusions, not progress toward the intended mechanism.

**Bottom line:** useful reproducibility, provenance, and a few bounded
negatives, but no new plaintext, key, intended operation, or confirmed in-game
clue. There has been no cryptanalytic breakthrough.
