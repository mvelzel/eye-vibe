# Forty-eighth pass — direct Petri Eye attribution

## New primary evidence

An original Discord message, not merely a forwarded quotation, is available
at:

<https://discord.com/channels/453998283174576133/626180507323072512/827647292805742683>

On 2 April 2021, `NollaArvi`, carrying the `Noita Developers` role, wrote:

> while Olli made the fungal shift and Petri did the eye symbol stuff

The surrounding conversation materially fixes the scope. Immediately before
the statement, NollaArvi said that Noita's secrets tend to be personal to the
developer who made them, so the other developers may not know their details.
He then gave examples:

- the Sun material was mostly his;
- the meditation-cube/End of Everything quest was largely Antti's;
- the fungal shift was Olli's;
- the Eye-symbol material was Petri's.

He followed the list with “just to give examples, mind you.” This qualifies
the list as examples rather than a complete ownership map; it does not weaken
the individual Eye attribution.

## What this establishes

The strongest supported reading is that Petri Purho authored the Eye secret,
not merely that he happened to publish the October 2020 release notes or might
work on a later addition.

“Eye symbol stuff” is informal and could distinguish the visible secret from
every internal step used to prepare its ciphertext. The conversation is
explicitly about ownership of game secrets, however, so treating Petri as the
primary Eye constructor is justified.

This supersedes the narrower provenance boundary in the forty-fifth pass,
which could identify Petri only as a candidate for later Eye/cauldron work.
It still does **not** attribute the Gate Guardian, cauldron, or any particular
later asset to Petri.

## Consequences for the search

1. Pre-October-2020 construction inputs should be available to Petri, not
   merely to some Nolla developer.
2. Petri-specific source, language, card/board-game, and coding archaeology
   becomes a justified filter. Resemblance alone remains insufficient; an
   operation must make a fixed Eye prediction.
3. Petri is Finnish, which makes Finnish construction material more plausible
   and is consistent with the independent `!Fi`/`358` header evidence. It does
   not select Finnish plaintext or a cipher.
4. Transfers from the cauldron, Gate Guardian, or another developer's secret
   now require evidence of Petri authorship or deliberate cross-developer
   coordination. Shared presence in Noita is not enough.
5. Later Petri-authored additions may still provide decoding clues even if
   they postdate the original ciphertext.

## Community-only boundary

A server-wide text search also finds later community recollections that Olli
made the cauldron and Petri made the Eyes. Those posts say “iirc” or ask the
claim as a question; they are not developer evidence and are not promoted.
The 2021 NollaArvi message directly supports only the Eye attribution and the
separate fungal-shift example.

## Decision

Promote Petri as the primary Eye-secret author. Reopen targeted construction
archaeology around Petri's pre-2020 materials and separately seek authorship
for proposed later clues. Do not use the attribution as a plaintext crib,
Finnish-language proof, or Gate mechanism.
