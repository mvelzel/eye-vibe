# Lymm — Wall Messages steganography practice

**Status:** Complete plaintext recovered; deterministic rule has two
source-bit defects; not author-confirmed.

## What was given

A prose cover resembling Noita's Wall Messages and the author hint “It's not a
Baconian.” The exact capitalization and punctuation are part of the cover.
No solution or later hint was inspected.

## Cracking method

The cover's awkward punctuation suggested treating each punctuation-delimited
clause as one code unit. Word-length was one of the frozen coarse observables.
Map each word of 1–3 letters to dot and each word of 4 or more letters to dash,
then read every clause as International Morse.

That immediately produced a long near-English stream and the exact suffix
`RUBEDOJUSTOUTOFREACH`. The remaining unexplained capitals occur inside
clauses. Splitting before each such capital makes them additional Morse
boundaries and explains every one of those typographic anomalies. Among the
natural length cutoffs and both bit polarities, the 3/4 split is the only
language-bearing result.

For example:

```text
Who do you worship         ...-   V
Who is                     ..     I
The one god                ...    S
Truly understand anything  ---    O
See who god is             ....   H
```

The visible rule decodes 168 of 170 word bits exactly. Alignment against the
resulting standard Morse gives a deletion-free defect certificate:

```text
group 16: word 37 "yet"   observed .  required -  => T
group 26: word 64 "this"  observed -  required .  => F
```

No visible rule independently selects those two exceptions, so they are
reported as carrier defects rather than hidden ingenuity.

## Full solution

```text
VISIONS OF ETERNITY LIE AHEAD
FULL OF HOPELESSNESS
RUBEDO JUST OUT OF REACH
```

Before the two minimal bit repairs, the raw decode is:


## Transferable lesson

Separate **group boundaries** from **bit carriers**. Punctuation and anomalous
capitalization can delimit symbols while a coarse lexical property supplies
the bits. This allows fluent cover prose to carry Morse without any repeated
cover phrase. The unchanged method fails on all 12 actual English Wall
Messages, so it is a learned attack pattern, not an in-game Eye clue.
